# Messaging channels are in scope

**Decided:** 2026-11-26, by the owner, following the request to include a Telegram-style channel experience in Hermes Mobile.

The owner later asked to use the phone app to view and set up supported messaging channels, including Telegram and WhatsApp. This supersedes the earlier decision that no messaging-channel UI belongs in the app.

**So:** Keep the dedicated Messaging channels destination under More, and keep channel credentials in that screen rather than the generic Keys and Advanced settings pages. Do not show saved secrets. Styling work must preserve the channel service's existing enable, test, restart and WhatsApp pairing behavior; enabling or changing a live channel is not part of visual redesign work.
