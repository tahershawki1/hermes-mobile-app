### 7.7 Phone performance benchmark

`tools/phone-benchmark.py` is a privacy-safe workstation benchmark for a connected Android phone. `tools/test_phone_benchmark.py` verifies its percentile calculations. The benchmark invokes every phone operation through Shizuku (`rish`), measures repeated dashboard health and plugin-route latency, samples Hermes Mobile process PSS and battery state, and reports median/P95 where a latency sample exists. It never records chat text, tokens, screenshots, identifiers, paths, or raw command output. Chat and tool-turn latency remain explicitly `not_measured` unless a websocket-capable runner is available; health and plugin HTTP timings must not be substituted for them. Run `python3 tools/phone-benchmark.py --runs 10 --output report.json` while holding the device lock.
